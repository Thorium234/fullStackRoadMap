Tournament Runner

Welcome to the Tournament Runner! This application allows you to manage and run tournaments with ease. Whether you're organizing a sports event, a gaming competition, or any other type of tournament, this tool will help you keep track of participants, matches, and results.

Features:

  • Create and manage tournaments
  • Add participants and teams
  • Schedule matches and track results
  • Generate brackets and standings

Get started by creating your first tournament and adding participants. Good luck, and may the best competitor win!
